# Social Media Strategy — Nhancio 2.0

## 1. Product Name & Category
**Product:** Nhancio 2.0
**Category:** AI Solutions Platform / Agency / Marketplace

## 2. Target Audience
**Who are we selling to?**
- Small to medium business owners looking for automation.
- Entrepreneurs building new AI products.
- Clients needing custom software and AI integration services.

## 3. Primary Distribution Channels
- **LinkedIn:** Building the brand's authority in the AI and automation space.
- **Twitter/X:** Sharing "Building in Public" updates and tech stack deep dives.
- **Product Hunt:** Launching new internal tools and platforms.
- **Medium/Substack:** Detailed articles on AI implementation and automation ROI.

## 4. Content Strategy
- **Building in Public:** Transparent updates on the development of Nhancio's ecosystem.
- **AI Roadmap Shares:** Showing where the company is headed and what new tools are coming.
- **Client Case Studies:** Highlighting the impact of custom solutions on client businesses.
- **Behind-the-Scenes:** Tech stack discussions and developer-focused content.

## 5. Growth Hack / Viral Loop
**The "Early Adopter Ecosystem" Loop:**
Offer a "Lifetime Discount" to the first 100 users of any new internal tool launched by Nhancio. Create a "Whitelabel Partner" program that allows other agencies to use Nhancio's core technology, creating a B2B network effect.
